Attention Commons

A working space for the idea that attention is a shared resource being enclosed by the attention economy — and for the counter-tradition that holds the wisdom about attention as something to be tended in common rather than sold.

It sits inside a broader frame, Culture of Attention: attention as one continuous field, from biology and the body to contemplative liberation, with the distraction industry at one end and practices of awakening at the other. The original "active solidarity / metacrisis" seed survives as one thread within this — the repo keeps its name, the centre of gravity has moved to attention.

## tracks

research/ — notes and source material: attention as a commons, commons and mutualism (Ostrom, Bollier, the P2P Foundation), the metacrisis debate (including its critics), principle-based organisations, neurophenomenology, the contemplative science of deep states, offline community forms, facilitation patterns.

concept/ — living documents on the emerging form: a "quasi-order" in Viktor's phrasing — shared principles, shared practices, a commons — with Buddhist-philosophical grounding translated into contemporary language.

## origin

Seeded from a conversation between Artem Zhiganov and Viktor Shiryaev on 20 April 2026 — docs/origin.md. How it sharpened into Attention Commons over the following month is in docs/development-log.md.

## status

Early and provisional. Nothing here is a finished position. A live thinking-space, not a publication.
