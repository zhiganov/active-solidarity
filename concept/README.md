## What this is now — Attention Commons / Culture of Attention

Between late April and late May 2026 the seed sharpened into a named frame (full story in [docs/development-log.md](../docs/development-log.md)):

- **Culture of Attention (Культура внимания)** — the umbrella. Viktor retired *Active Presence* and rebranded it Culture of Attention (transition ceremony 2026-05-10); it also holds his educational programs and a Buddhist track, *Marvelous Gates*.
- **Attention Commons** — the commons-and-governance arm, the part worked on jointly here. The claim: attention is a shared resource being enclosed by the attention economy, and there is a counter-tradition that holds the *wisdom about attention* as a commons.
- The **"active solidarity / metacrisis"** seed survives as one thread (via Rufus Pollock), now nested under attention rather than the top-level frame.

**The central conceptual move** is the resolution of the bounded-commons problem: attention is universal but commons are local, so the *commons* is the contemplative community and the *transmitted wisdom about attention* — with a layered reading where commons (governable) sits below attention-as-ground (only recognisable). See `research/`.

**The strategic discipline** (to carry into every document):
- Keep the *civilizational frame* (why it matters beyond the people involved) and the *practical Ostromian questions* (what this practice does to a life and a community; whether it resists commodification; what governance preserves substance) **distinguishable**. Let the practical questions inform the frame — not the frame answer the practical questions.
- Work the **three "pink zones"** of the strategic map (collective everyday stewardship; shared everyday language for attentiveness; the science of deep states) — *not* "another meditation app" or "another mindfulness school."

## Working form — "Sōka Gakkai in a new key"

Viktor's phrase, adopted as working-title shape. A quasi-order — not a religion, not a company, not a school — built around:

- **Shared principles** (not values — see below)
- **Shared practices** — concrete, downloadable, runnable offline by anyone without gatekeepers
- **A commons** — open materials; Patreon / donation / grant-funded rather than course-by-course
- **Local autonomous cells** — each one holds the principles and develops its own adjacent work (ecological, contemplative, organisational, civic), loosely federated via the shared ground

Reference forms: Order of Interbeing (Plum Village), Alcoholics Anonymous, Russian Old Believers. All principle-based, all durable, none centrally controlled.

## Principles vs values

This distinction matters and should show up in everything.

- **Values** ≈ "it is nice when X, I prefer X." Fragile — put them under pressure (prison, war, scarcity) and they dissolve into emotion and self-preservation.
- **Principles** ≈ "under pressure we still do X because without X this breaks." Load-bearing.

Contemporary exemplar: AA's 12 Steps. Historical: Old Believer work ethic + mutual aid. Both survive pressure because they are principled, not sentimental.

## Tone

"Guerrilla activism" (Viktor) — open kit, partisan distribution. Seed envelopes buried in gardens. Meditation cards in mailboxes. Nothing that requires permission or a gatekeeper. The form is a lay-accessible analogue of what Nichiren did with chanting the title of the *Lotus Sutra*: a simple concrete practice plus a principled commitment, both portable, both repeatable.

## The manifesto

Viktor drafted a manifesto for **Culture of Attention** (originally for the introduction and conclusion of a book he is writing). Its moves: a culture of attention *exists* — rooted in biology and the body, reaching to contemplative liberation, with everything in between (attention economy, distraction, mundane concentration); one field explored at its edges across all of civilization (Ptahhotep → the Buddha → Kant → modern neuroscience); we have the word "attention economy" but not "culture of attention"; without a culture of attention the other useful cultures (hygiene, mental health, civic life) don't hold; so make it *visible* as a commons, study how contemplative traditions govern their commons, and free attention as a public resource. Viktor flagged the polished version as imperfect and book-bound; its key moves are recorded in the [development log](../docs/development-log.md) and the finished text remains his to publish.
